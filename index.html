Why Zipper

Travelling well is a craft. We've been practising since 2011.

We started Zipper after a decade of running group trips that felt rushed and interchangeable. Ours are neither. Routes are walked first by our team, guides are locals, and groups stay small enough that every meal feels like a dinner with friends.

  • ✓Small groups of 10–14 — never a coach in sight.
  • ✓Local guides on every single departure.
