Our Story

Guides first, groups second.

We're a small team of former guides, journalists and lifelong wanderers who believe travel is a craft — and has been treated like a product for too long.

From one backpacks' worth

Zipper started on a trail, in 2011, with eleven friends.

Our founder, Naima Voss, was leading treks in the Peruvian highlands when a group of strangers asked her to plan "the trip their guidebook ruined." She walked them off the map instead. That first, unadvertised route became this company.

Fifteen years on, the rule hasn't changed: we never sell a route we haven't walked. Every departure is tested by our own team, led by a local, and capped at fourteen guests so that dinner is still a conversation.

What We Stand For

The compass points we never argue with.

Small, on purpose

Ten to fourteen guests, always. Big enough to share, small enough to be real.

Local, always

Guides who grew up there, family-run stays, and money that stays in the place.

Walked first

No route is ever sold before our team has hiked, eaten and slept their way through it.

Slow by default

Fewer destinations, more days in each. The itinerary flexes, never the travellers.
